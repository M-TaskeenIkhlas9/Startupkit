Employee access

You confirm

Decide what each new hire can see inside StartupKit. Start narrower than feels necessary — it's easy to grant more access later, harder to walk back after someone's already seen the cap table.

Access tiers

Full Access
Sees and edits everything — cap table, legal docs, financials, every module. For a co-founder or CTO/CFO-level hire.
Module Access
Sees and edits only assigned modules — e.g. an engineer gets W4 only. Everything else stays hidden, not just read-only.
View Only
Sees high-level status and progress, but can't open underlying documents or financials. A safe early default.
No Access
Not on the platform. Fine for most early hourly or contract hires.
