# Employment Agreement

### Acme AI, Inc.

This Employment Agreement (this "Agreement") is entered into as of July 21, 2026, by and between
**Acme AI, Inc.**, a Delaware C-Corporation (the "Company"), and
**Jordan Lee**, an individual residing at 123 Market St, San Francisco, CA 94103 ("Employee").

## 1 · Position and duties

The Company employs Employee as **Senior Software Engineer**, reporting to **the CEO**, beginning on
**August 4, 2026**. Employee will perform the duties customary to the role and such other duties as
reasonably assigned, will devote their full business time and best efforts to the Company, and will
comply with the Company's policies as in effect from time to time.

## 2 · At-will employment

Employment with the Company is **at will**. Either Employee or the Company may terminate the
employment relationship at any time, with or without cause and with or without notice. Nothing in
this Agreement creates a contract of employment for any fixed term. The at-will nature of the
relationship may only be changed in a writing signed by Employee and an authorized officer of the
Company.

## 3 · Compensation

- **Classification:** Full-time, exempt.
- **Base salary:** **$140,000** per year, less applicable withholdings and deductions, payable
  semi-monthly in accordance with the Company's standard payroll practices.
- **Review:** Employee's compensation may be reviewed and adjusted at the Company's discretion.

## 4 · Equity

25,000 options — any equity is a separate grant, subject to the Company's equity incentive plan, the
applicable grant documents, and a vesting schedule approved by the Board (customarily four years
with a one-year cliff). This Agreement does not itself grant any equity.

## 5 · Benefits and paid time off

- **Benefits:** Health, dental & vision; 401(k); $500/mo home-office stipend, subject to the terms of the applicable plans, which the
  Company may amend or terminate at any time.
- **Paid time off:** Unlimited PTO + company holidays, subject to Company policy.

## 6 · Confidential information and inventions

As a condition of employment, Employee must sign the Company's **Proprietary Information and
Inventions Agreement ("PIIA")**, which governs confidentiality and the assignment of inventions.
The PIIA is incorporated into this Agreement by reference and survives the end of employment.

## 7 · Outside activities and conflicts

During employment, Employee will not engage in any activity that conflicts or competes with the
Company's business or interferes with Employee's duties, without the Company's prior written
consent. Employee represents that they are under no obligation to any third party that would
conflict with this Agreement.

## 8 · Work authorization

This offer of employment is contingent on Employee's satisfactory completion of **Form I-9** and
verification of authorization to work in the United States within the time required by law.

## 9 · Termination

On termination for any reason, Employee will receive all earned but unpaid base salary through the
last day of employment and any other amounts required by law, and will promptly return all Company
property and Confidential Information. Sections 6, 7, and 10–12 survive termination.

## 10 · Governing law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of **California**, and Employee's
employment is subject to the employment laws of **California**, without regard to
conflict-of-law rules.

## 11 · Entire agreement

This Agreement, together with the PIIA, the offer letter, and any equity grant documents,
constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding Employee's employment and supersedes
all prior discussions. It may be amended only in a writing signed by both parties.

## 12 · Acknowledgement

Employee acknowledges that they have read and understood this Agreement, have had the opportunity to
seek independent counsel, and enter into it voluntarily.

## 📤 What to do next

Confirm the base salary and classification are correct (exempt vs. non-exempt affects overtime),
then send this to the new hire for signature **together with the PIIA**. Do not let the employee
begin work until both are signed and **Form I-9** is completed within the legal deadline. Keep the
executed copies in the employee's personnel file.

> Draft modeled on a standard U.S. at-will employment agreement. Not legal advice — employment law
> is state-specific (final-pay timing, non-compete/non-solicit enforceability, and required notices
> vary by state). Have counsel review before use.
