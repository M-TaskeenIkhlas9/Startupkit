==============================================================================
 LEGEND — How to read the 8 Startup Launch Workflow diagrams
==============================================================================

Each diagram is a fine-grained, end-to-end user journey. Read it top to bottom.
Every box is a single action performed by a single actor. The vertical bands
(swimlanes) tell you WHO performs each action.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 SWIMLANES — the actors (vertical columns)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FOUNDER
   A human action taken in the platform's UI (clicking, entering data,
   reviewing, approving, signing). In Workflow 4 this lane is labelled "CTO"
   (the technical founder).

PLATFORM
   An automated action performed by the system we are building — generating
   documents, pre-filling forms, calling APIs, running checks, storing files.

THIRD-PARTY
   An action performed by, or a wait on, an EXTERNAL service or person that
   the platform calls out to. This is NOT part of our system.
   "Third-Party" includes, but is NOT limited to:
       - A hired partner attorney / outside legal counsel
       - Government bodies: Delaware Secretary of State, IRS, state tax offices
       - Formation / registered-agent services: Stripe Atlas, Firstbase
       - Banks & fintech: Mercury, Relay, Ramp, Brex
       - Payments & accounting: Stripe, QuickBooks, Xero
       - E-signature: DocuSign
       - Payroll / HR: Gusto, Rippling, Deel
       - Infrastructure vendors: Cloudflare, GitHub, Doppler, Supabase, Neon,
         Clerk, Auth0, Resend, Postmark, Sentry, Better Uptime, AWS, GCP,
         Vercel, Railway
       - GTM / sales tools: DocSend, HubSpot, Attio, Apollo.io, Clay, Intercom
       - Workspace & ops: Google Workspace, Slack, 1Password, Calendly, Loom,
         Zoom, Zapier, Make
   In short: any party outside the platform — whether a paid human professional
   (like a partner attorney) or an external software/API — lives in this lane.

EMPLOYEE  (Workflow 6 only)
   A new hire's action during onboarding (e.g. entering direct-deposit details,
   completing tax forms).

DESIGN TOOL  (Workflow 5 only)
   Asset/output produced via a design tool (e.g. Figma) — logo files,
   design tokens.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 SHAPES & COLOURS — what each node means
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

WHITE rounded box ........ Process step. One actor, one atomic action.

YELLOW diamond ........... Decision. Always a yes/no question. Two arrows
                           leave it (e.g. "yes" / "no"). Three-way choices are
                           shown as two diamonds in a row.

BLUE box ................. WAIT STATE. The flow is paused for an external event
                           or a time delay (e.g. "WAIT — 1 to 5 business days",
                           "WAIT — all founders sign"). Nothing happens until
                           the event occurs.

ORANGE box ............... Document / asset produced and stored (an artefact
                           that leaves the system — e.g. Certificate of
                           Incorporation, signed PIIA, invoice template, logo).

RED box .................. Failure, retry, or time-critical step. Marks error
                           handling paths and irreversible deadlines
                           (e.g. the 83(b) election — file within 30 days,
                           no extensions).

GREEN ball / box ......... Start of the flow, or a track marked complete/GREEN
                           (used in the parallel tracks of Workflows 4 and 8).

PURPLE box ............... Cross-workflow trigger or gate. Marks where one
                           workflow hands off to (or is unblocked by) another.


------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 SYMBOLS used in labels
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  TRIGGER (down arrow) ... This workflow has reached a milestone that UNBLOCKS
                           another workflow. Example: W1 "EIN received" unblocks
                           W3's bank-account step.

  ENTER (up/left arrow) .. This workflow STARTS (or resumes) because an upstream
                           workflow reached a milestone. Example: W2 enters on
                           "entity formed" from W1.

  Loops .................. An arrow that bends back upward is a loop — the same
                           steps repeat (e.g. once per founder, once per new
                           hire, or "edit and re-check until approved").

  Fork / Join ............ Where one arrow splits into several parallel columns
                           that run at the same time, then merge back together.
                           Workflows 4 and 8 are the most parallel-heavy.
