AI Co-Founder

Grounded Q&A: the founder asks a question, the system retrieves the relevant slice of their Company Object, asks the LLM with that context, and returns an answer plus suggested actions that link into workflows. The same grounded-chat pattern also powers the in-workflow assistants (idea, brand, GTM).
