Knowledge graph

The Company Object made visible: a central node ringed by clusters — Founder, Idea, Founders, Domains, Documents, Your answers, Saved facts, Inputs — each a projection of the same underlying record. Clicking a cluster inspects its leaves. It's a view, not a store.
