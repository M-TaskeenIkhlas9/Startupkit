Why this matters even on self-managed Postgres. A platform that captures EIN/SSN to fill formation and tax forms sits in tax-preparer territory under the FTC Safeguards Rule — encryption at rest and in transit isn't optional. Disk encryption alone doesn't help if a DB credential leaks, so PII gets application-layer envelope encryption with keys held outside the database. There's no fixed legal retention period, so we set and enforce one (e.g. purge raw EIN/SSN after filing, or on request) — easy because it lives in its own table. Shared deployment.
