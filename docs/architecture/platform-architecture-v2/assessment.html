Assessment questionnaire

A structured intake driven entirely by a catalog (phases → modules → questions) that lives as data, not code. Answers are saved per phase and become inputs to the health score, the knowledge graph's "answers" cluster, and the journey. Add a question by editing the catalog — no schema change.
