The 7 Layers
A startup's complete infrastructure sits across seven distinct layers, each of which has to exist before the company can operate sustainably. The layers aren't optional — missing any one of them creates a compounding problem later.
Layer 1 — Legal & Compliance. The legal backbone. Covers company formation (filing the entity, getting your EIN, issuing founder shares), cap table and equity management (who owns what, on what vesting schedule), legal document automation (SAFEs, convertible notes, NDAs, IP assignments), and compliance certifications (SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR — required before most enterprise customers will buy from you).
Layer 2 — Finance & Banking. Everything money-related: a business bank account, corporate cards and expense management, bookkeeping and accounting, invoicing and payment processing, financial planning and runway modeling, and investor relations tooling (secure pitch deck sharing, investor updates).
Layer 3 — Technical Infrastructure. The foundation the product sits on: domain and DNS, cloud hosting, version control, CI/CD pipeline, database, authentication, transactional email, file storage and CDN, monitoring and error tracking, secrets management, and security/vulnerability scanning. This layer runs 24/7 and is invisible when working correctly.
Layer 4 — Product & Development. How the team builds: project and issue tracking, design and prototyping, no-code website and landing pages, product analytics, feature flags and A/B testing, and technical/user-facing documentation.
Layer 5 — People & HR. How the team is managed: payroll and benefits (US domestic and global), applicant tracking for hiring, performance management and OKRs, and IT/MDM for device provisioning and app access.
Layer 6 — Customer, Sales & Marketing. How revenue is generated: CRM and pipeline management, customer support and helpdesk, email marketing, outbound sales tooling, web and product analytics, SEO, and payment/subscription monetisation.
Layer 7 — Operations & Communications. The connective tissue: team messaging, email and workspace suite, scheduling and video conferencing, password and access management, identity and SSO, and workflow automation that ties all the other layers together.
